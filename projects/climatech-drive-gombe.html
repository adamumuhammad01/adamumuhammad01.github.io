Key Project

Climatech Drive Gombe

Sep 2023 – Feb 2024 $500 grant, Meridian International Centre, USA Programme Manager (implementation support)

Overview

A youth climate-action training programme delivered across Gombe State, supported by a $500 grant from the Meridian International Centre, USA.

Activities

  • Managed and supported programme implementation with the project team.
  • Trained 250+ youth in climate action advocacy in northern Nigeria.
  • Conducted 5 climate action projects in Gombe State.
