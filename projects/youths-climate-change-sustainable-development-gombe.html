Key Project

Youths, Climate Change, and Sustainable Development in Gombe State

10–19 Jun 2023 Sustainable Development Solutions Network Project designer and lead

Overview

A project mobilising students from high schools across Gombe Metropolis to learn about the Sustainable Development Goals, run with a team of SDGs Advocates.

Activities

  • Designed and led the project.
  • Mobilised students from high schools within Gombe Metropolis, together with a team of SDGs Advocates, to learn about the SDGs.
